Our Story

Tashvi Collective was founded with a simple vision - to bring the timeless beauty of Indian craftsmanship into modern homes and wardrobes around the world.

Based in London, Tashvi Collective combines traditional artisan techniques with contemporary designs to create thoughtfully crafted home and fashion products. Every collection celebrates India's rich textile heritage while offering products that are practical, elegant, and made to be cherished.

The company was founded by Neha Akash Kedia, originally from Jaipur, Rajasthan, who moved to London in 2016, and Neha Ashok Kurani, originally from Nagpur, Maharashtra, who moved to London in 2014. United by a shared passion for handcrafted textiles and ethical craftsmanship, they established Tashvi Collective to connect skilled Indian artisans with customers who appreciate authentic, high-quality handmade products.

Our collections include premium cotton clothing, bathrobes, bedsheets, table linens, handcrafted bags, and home décor, carefully sourced and created with attention to quality, sustainability, and timeless design.

At Tashvi Collective, every purchase supports traditional craftsmanship, empowers artisan communities, and helps preserve generations-old techniques for the future.
